Job Title: Senior AI & Machine Learning Engineer / Data Scientist
Company: Apex Innovations
Location: San Francisco, CA / Remote

Role Overview:
Apex Innovations is seeking an experienced Senior AI & Machine Learning Engineer to design, train, and deploy enterprise AI solutions. You will be instrumental in building state-of-the-art Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Model (LLM) applications, and production machine learning pipelines.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and deploy production-grade machine learning and deep learning models using Python, PyTorch, and Scikit-Learn.
- Develop advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) solutions including text classification, information extraction, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with vector databases.
- Fine-tune and evaluate Large Language Models (LLMs) using modern frameworks such as Hugging Face, Transformers, and LangChain.
- Implement robust MLOps, containerization with Docker and Kubernetes, and automated CI/CD deployment pipelines on AWS or GCP.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including software engineers, product managers, and data engineers.
- Write clean, maintainable, modular code supported by thorough unit testing and agile development methodologies.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- 3+ years of professional experience in Machine Learning, Data Science, or AI engineering.
- Deep hands-on proficiency in Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-Learn.
- Demonstrated experience building deep learning systems with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Strong practical knowledge of Natural Language Processing (NLP), LLMs, Transformers, and RAG architectures.
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS preferred, including SageMaker, S3, EC2).
- Containerization and orchestration experience using Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience with Git, GitHub, CI/CD, and automated unit testing (Pytest).
- Excellent communication, critical thinking, problem solving, and team collaboration skills.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or related STEM discipline.

Nice to Have:
- Knowledge of Vector Databases (ChromaDB, Pinecone, or FAISS).
- Experience with PySpark or BigQuery for large-scale data processing.
- Published research or open-source AI project contributions.
