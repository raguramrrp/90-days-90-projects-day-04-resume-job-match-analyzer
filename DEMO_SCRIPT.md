## ⏱️ Video Demonstration Sequence

### [00:00 - 00:20] The Problem & The Solution
> "Hello! Today, 75% of resumes are filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human recruiter ever sees them. Candidates struggle to know whether their resume actually aligns with a job description.
> For Day 4 of my 90 Days 90 Projects challenge, I engineered an **AI-Powered Resume–Job Description Compatibility Analyzer** that evaluates document compatibility, detects critical keyword gaps, audits action verbs and impact metrics, and provides an actionable optimization roadmap."

### [00:20 - 00:45] Document Input & Benchmark Presets
> "The dashboard supports direct upload of PDF, Word (.docx), and plain text documents, with robust text cleaning, contact info extraction, and section indexing.
> Let's test it with one of our benchmark presets: 'Senior AI Engineer vs Senior AI/Data Scientist Job Description'.
> Notice how the dual-pane editor instantly populates with real resume and job posting data. We also have customizable algorithm weight sliders in the sidebar for tailored ATS tuning."

### [00:45 - 01:15] Running Compatibility Analysis & Visual Scorecard
> "Clicking **'Run Compatibility Analysis'** invokes our multi-tier NLP pipeline:
> 1. First, an interactive **Plotly Radial Gauge** displays our composite ATS Score — calibrated across skill keyword coverage, TF-IDF semantic cosine similarity, quantifiable impact metrics, and structural completeness.
> 2. The **Domain Skill Radar Chart** compares the candidate's technical profile against target expectations across 8 core industry domains.
> 3. The **Top Weighted Keywords Chart** highlights high-value n-grams extracted via TF-IDF."

### [01:15 - 01:40] Deep Skill Gap Matrix & Actionable ATS Recommendations
> "Switching to the **Skill Gap Matrix**, we see an exact inventory:
> - **Matched Skills:** Python, PyTorch, Docker, Kubernetes, LangChain, AWS.
> - **Prioritized Missing Skills:** High-impact missing keywords like 'Faiss', 'Pinecone', and 'PySpark'.
> Next, in the **Recommendations Tab**, the engine conducts an action-verb audit and quantifiable impact assessment, urging the candidate to replace passive phrasing and quantify achievements using the Google XYZ formula."

### [01:40 - 02:00] Standalone Export & Wrap-Up
> "Finally, under the **Export Tab**, candidates can download a self-contained, publication-ready **HTML Scorecard** or structured **JSON payload**.
> The project is 100% test-covered with 26 automated unit tests, fully documented, and ready for immediate deployment on Streamlit Cloud. Thank you!"
